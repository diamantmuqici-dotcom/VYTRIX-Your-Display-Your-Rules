How it works

Three moves, no ceremony.

Install, pick a display, bind a look. The app keeps the before state so you never have to guess what changed.

01

Choose a display

VYTRIX lists connected monitors by identifier. Work on one without touching the others.

02

Tune or load

Adjust the sliders yourself or load a profile from the gallery. Save it under a name you will recognise.

03

Bind and revert

Attach it to a game executable, set a hotkey, and restore the snapshot whenever you want.
